# Questions for the Supervisor

Bring these questions to the first proposal meeting. They are deliberately decision-oriented so that scope, ethics and evaluation can be fixed early.

1. Is the proposed non-clinical framing—benchmarking chatbot response behaviours in safe synthetic conversations—suitable, or should the project avoid the term “psychosis” in its title and use “high-certainty/unverified interpretations” instead?
2. Does the department require formal ethics approval, an ethics self-assessment, or only supervisor approval for a synthetic model-only study with independent annotators?
3. Would an adviser with mental-health expertise need to review scenario categories and the annotation codebook before pilot, and how should that review be arranged?
4. Is the proposed recommended scale (24 scenarios, four endpoints, three runs and 96 double-coded trajectories) realistic for the dissertation timeframe, or should the MVP be the formal project target?
5. Does the programme expect a design-science framing, a controlled experiment framing, or a stated mixed-methods/artefact-evaluation approach?
6. Are the four proposed research questions sufficiently focused and measurable for the marking criteria? Which one, if any, should be removed?
7. Is the distinction between emotional validation and endorsement a sufficiently novel and defensible benchmark contribution, and what evidence should support it?
8. Which model providers/endpoints are acceptable under university policy, budget and storage rules? Is a local/open-weight model required or merely desirable?
9. What standard of annotation review is expected: two independent raters, a supervisor/subject expert check, or both? Is recruiting annotators permitted?
10. May the code, aggregate results, scenario templates and raw model outputs be released in an appendix/Git repository, and what redaction/provider-term checks are required?
11. What dissertation word limit, required chapter structure, reference style and deadline apply? None of these were found in the inspected research-methods folders.
12. What declaration of generative-AI assistance, if any, is required for planning, code support, editing and literature-search assistance?

The need to clarify requirements, risks, outcomes and method before implementation is **adapted from the Research Methods material** — `6.1Proposals.pdf`, pp. 9–21; `week 89  ProjectMgmt.pptx`, slides 6–17. The specific questions are **your additional recommendation**.
